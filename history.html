Colombian Aerospace Agency


The Colombian Aerospace Agency, was officially established in 2018 as a national aerospace initiave focused on strengthening Colombian technolpgical independence and scientific leadership in Latin America. The project was led by aerospace pioner and private investor Hanner Palacios , who vision was to develop a sustainable orbital program built on Colombia engineering talent.


In 2016, preliminary research began through university partnership in Bogota and Medellin focusing on propulsion system, lightweight composite material, and small satelite platforms. By 2019, the agency completed its first experimental atmospferic research rocket, making Colombia, initial step toward launch vehicle development


Betewn 2020 and 2023, The Agency invested heavily in propulsion testing facilities, guidance system, and reusable laung technologies. Under the strategic direction of Hanner Palacios, the agency secured privated secton funding to accelerate the development of the Andes launch vehicle, Colombia first cenceptual orbit-class rocket


Today, the agency continues advancing in propulsion engineering, satellite deployment system and aerospace research. What began as a visionary investment has involved into a national engineering movement-positioning Colombia for its future beyond the earth
